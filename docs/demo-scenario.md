# ServiceTrail demo scenario

## Company

BrightFix is a small home-appliance repair company with one supervisor and three technicians. Customers usually call or message the company. The office creates a service job so the technician's visit and the follow-up do not disappear into chat.

## Scenario: noisy ceiling fan

1. A customer reports that a ceiling fan is making a loud noise.
2. The supervisor creates a service request with the customer's contact details, address, and the original wording.
3. The supervisor assigns the job to a technician for the next available visit.
4. The technician starts the visit and records the arrival.
5. The technician records the observation: the fan makes a loud noise at medium speed and the mounting is loose.
6. The technician attaches a before photo and records the work performed: mounting tightened and fan tested.
7. The technician records the outcome as **further work required** because a replacement capacitor is needed.
8. The technician submits the service report.
9. The supervisor reviews the report and asks for the missing after photo.
10. The technician adds the after photo and resubmits.
11. The supervisor approves the operational record and records customer/site confirmation.
12. ServiceTrail creates a follow-up action for the replacement part and assigns it to the supervisor.
13. After the follow-up visit, the company records the final work and closes the job.
14. The customer and equipment history show the complete service trail.

## What the demo proves

- The office can see what the technician actually recorded.
- Evidence is attached to the correct service job.
- Incomplete reports return for correction without erasing history.
- Follow-up responsibility is explicit.
- A later technician can understand the previous visit.
- The system records work; it does not pretend to diagnose the fan automatically.
